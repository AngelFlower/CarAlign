CarAlign is a music player that fixes something every factory car stereo gets wrong: the driver sits off-center, so the sound from the nearer speaker arrives first and the stage drifts toward the door.

Add songs from your phone and play them in CarAlign. Run the automatic alignment: the phone's microphone measures how much earlier one side arrives (usually around 1 ms) and CarAlign delays that channel so the music centers in front of you. No setup, no special permissions.

Built for factory stereos with Android Auto or Bluetooth that have no delay adjustment.

What it does
• Built-in player: queue, seek, notification and lock-screen controls.
• Automatic closed-loop alignment: measure, apply and verify, with audible cues so you don't have to look at the screen.
• Analysis by band (bass, mid, high), clock-drift correction and motion rejection using the gyroscope.
• Manual fine adjustment in 0.02 ms steps, applied live while music plays.
• Demo that steps the delay from 0 to your setting so you can hear the stage center.
• Saved profiles (driver, passenger…).
• Diagnostics report you can copy to share.

Advanced: capture mode
• Delay the audio of Tidal, Spotify or any app that allows audio capture, muting the original so you hear it once.
• Safety breaker that gives the sound back if an app blocks capture.
• Needs an ADB channel or Shizuku on the phone (enabled from a computer with adb tcpip 5555 after each reboot). The built-in player doesn't need any of this.

No accounts, no ads, nothing leaves your phone. Open source (MIT).
